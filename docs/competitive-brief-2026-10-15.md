# KopaBook competitive brief (Nigeria, verified Oct 15 2026)

Why this exists: you set the KopaBook vision today (free record book for Nigerian local businesses, offline-first, WhatsApp-native, single source of truth). This grounds the positioning against what actually exists in the Nigerian market, so the pitch and build order are aimed at a real gap, not a guess.

## The market size

Nigeria has about 41 million MSMEs contributing roughly 50 percent of GDP (Statista, via thecondia.com). The typical small business "system" today is handwritten notebooks, voice notes, Instagram DM screenshots and mental maths (allbiohub.com review of Bumpa, Sep 2026). The pain is real and documented.

## The incumbent: Bumpa

Bumpa (founded 2021, Kelvin Umechukwu and Adetunji Opayele) is Africa's leading business management platform for retail, built exactly for Nigerian merchants.

- Scale: 60,000 to 70,000 Nigerian small and medium businesses served; over NGN 160 billion in transactions processed since launch (businessday.ng, techcabal.com, 2025).
- What it does: free online store builder, inventory and order management, online and offline payments, invoicing and receipts, bookkeeping, dispatch rider requests, customer engagement via bulk SMS and email, business analytics, multi-currency (dignited.com, thecondia.com).
- Free plan: free online store, unlimited products and orders, SSL, SEO, WhatsApp and SMS notifications, friendly analytics, payment/logistics/social integrations. Catch: 1.5 percent charge on online transactions; advanced logistics (Shipbubble delivery choice) sits on the Pro and Growth plans (dignited.com; businessday.ng sponsored, 2026).
- Bumpa also bought PaperCloud and relaunched it as Paperless, its invoicing app: NGN 2,000 per month, NGN 6,000 bi-annual, NGN 10,000 annual, 14-day free trial (techcabal.com, Feb 2025).

## Where KopaBook wins (the honest gaps)

1. Almost without network. Bumpa is an online-store-centric product. KopaBook's offline-first PWA (records save on the device first, sync when network returns) is the one feature a trader in a low-network market feels every day. Nothing in Bumpa's public positioning claims this.
2. Free book, not free store. Bumpa monetizes through transaction charges and paid tiers. KopaBook's core record book free forever, with paid power features only at the edges (multi-branch, chatbot tier, advanced analytics), is a cleaner trust story for merchants who fear fees.
3. Source of truth, not just a storefront. Bumpa organizes selling. KopaBook's review queue (unclear entries quarantined, never silently corrupting the books), supplier profiles with verification flags, and community trade ratings are a different product: the book the owner trusts, not the shop window.
4. Any business, not just product sellers. Services mode for artisans and professionals (jobs, hours, materials) plus buy-versus-sell margin for resellers covers traders Bumpa's retail framing leaves out.
5. WhatsApp-native, free tier first. Bumpa lists WhatsApp notifications. KopaBook's free layer (one-tap sale receipts via wa.me links, automatic debt reminders on WhatsApp, structured message recording) meets traders in the app they already live in, before any paid Cloud API chatbot tier.

## Marketplace imports: what is verified and what is not

Verified: Jumia's Seller Hub gives sellers inventory management, analytics, sales reporting and performance tracking (fastercapital.com summary of Jumia Seller Hub). Jumia Seller Centre also exposes export flows for product templates (jumia-global.com seller college). Konga sellers can monitor sales, pending, shipped and cancelled orders from their own account (techmoran.com, 2014). Konga shows sellers their successful-sales counts and buyer ratings on listings (techpoint.africa).

NOT verified from public docs: a one-tap CSV download of sales reports from either platform. The import format must be checked against a real Jumia or Konga seller account before we promise it. Until then, phase one is "import your marketplace sales reports" with the exact format confirmed at build time, not "one-tap CSV" as a guarantee.

## Suggested one-line positioning

Bumpa is the online store for Nigerian merchants. KopaBook is the record book: free, works without network, and tells the owner the truth about their money.
